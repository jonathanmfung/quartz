I've been on the search for games to speedrun. I would like the runs to be fairly short (<30 min, possibly 1hr max) and not be too mechanically demanding. For some reason I want to play on my (Android) phone, which means consoles on the older side (N64 - Wii) are easier to access. It would also be more satisfying to be an Any% or 100% run, in contrast to a specific level or section.

Here's my current list:
- Mega Man Zero 1 (GBA) - ~17m Any%, ~35m All Missions
    - Fairly precise movement and inputs, Complex combat, Some RNG manipulation
    - https://megamanleaderboards.net/index.php?game=25&category=388
    - [Flameberger is the certified expert](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLHP5kgfrdZVIwUUwuWcq1qZ9HxqveynkG)
- Arctic Tale (GBA) - ~5m Any%
    - Fairly casual, Routing focused
    - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Slx2936WKk
