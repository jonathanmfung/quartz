- How do you wash the dishes? Do you use the dishwasher or just a drying rack? Do you soak before you scrub? Do you keep the water running?
- When you use chopsticks, how do you get the last pieces in a bowl, is bringing the bowl up to your mouth and shoveling okay? Do you rest your chopsticks inside the bowl or on the rim?
- How do you regulate temperature inside your home? Windows, fans, doors, air conditioning, swamp coolers, or space heaters?
- Do you put your seatbelt on before driving a car?
- Do you split your laundry into colors, darks, and whites?
