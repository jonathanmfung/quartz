A good portion of the media I consume are livestreams and livestream VODs[^1]. How much I actively watch the stream versus use it as background sound varies; regardless, my consumption behavior can be regarded as wholemeal.

I am reminded of a Twitter thread I saw a couple months ago.[^2] The original poster claimed that they read all the classic books by asking some LLM for summaries. Of course, someone responded with vehement disagreement. They took the position that _reading_ is more than just understanding the plot.

Livestreams vs their clips
Sports games vs their highlights
Books vs cliff notes
Movies vs synopses

Events vs Headlines

Being an expert. Or maybe it's just about immersing yourself in the process.

[^1]: Formally, the acronym means 'Video On Demand', but in this context it refers to recorded versions of livestreams.

[^2]: It most definitely was ragebait, but that's besides the point.
