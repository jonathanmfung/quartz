The first scene of [ITZY's Girls Will Be Girls music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1KhOhW_O8-k) shows a huge flock of crows. Just afterwards, however, are these creatures shown in closer detail-- they are large eyeballs with wings.

I immediately recognized these creatures as [flyeyes](https://maplestorywiki.net/w/Flyeye) from [[maplestory]].[^1] Given the early-2000s prevalence of Maplestory in Korea, I do not doubt the creative team had this exact sprite on their moodboard. However, I was still wondering if this particular depiction of a flying eyeball had other cultural influence and symbolism.

It seems that in America, most know this creature from mid-20th century illustrations by [Von Dutch](https://web.archive.org/web/20250517060941/https://lethalthreat.com/blogs/news/von-dutch-and-the-history-of-the-flying-eyeball) and later [Rick Griffin](https://web.archive.org/web/20250703002507/https://www.bonhams.com/auction/16257/lot/87/rick-griffin-the-flying-eyeball/). Griffin's version seems to be more prevalent, due to his work for the rock band the [Grateful Dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20240815215108/https://www.gdao.org/exhibits/show/posters/rick-griffin).

Michael Bukowski has also [asked this same question](https://web.archive.org/web/20250618075827/http://yog-blogsoth.blogspot.com/2022/04/eyewing.html), specifically using the name 'eyewing'.

Floating eyeballs sans-wings are another variant:
- Marvel's [Shuma-Gorath](https://www.marvel.com/characters/shuma-gorath)
- Dungeons & Dragons' [Beholder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beholder_(Dungeons_%26_Dragons))
- Kirby's [Kracko](https://wikirby.com/wiki/Kracko), [Zero](https://wikirby.com/wiki/Zero), and [0²](https://wikirby.com/wiki/0%C2%B2)
- The Iglulik Inuit's[Issitôq](https://abookofcreatures.com/2019/02/15/issitoq/)[^2]


[^1]: Flyeyes are exclusive to a map called [_The Passage_](https://maplestory.io/api/KMS/389/map/211041900/render).

[^2]: See also: [Intellectual culture of the Hudson Bay Eskimos book page 176 & 192 (Rasmussen, 1930)](https://archive.org/details/intellectualcult00rasm/page/n217/mode/2up?q=issitoq)
