The first scene of [ITZY's Girls Will Be Girls music video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1KhOhW_O8-k) shows a huge flock of crows. Just afterwards, however, are these creatures shown in closer detail-- they are large eyeballs with wings.

I immediately recognized these creatures as [flyeyes](https://maplestorywiki.net/w/Flyeye) from [[maplestory]]. Given the historic prevalence of Maplestory in Korea, I do not doubt the creative team had this exact sprite on their moodboard. However, I was still wondering if this particular depiction of a flying eyeball had other cultural influence and symbolism.
