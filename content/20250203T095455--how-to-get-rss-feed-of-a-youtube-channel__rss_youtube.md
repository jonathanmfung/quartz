Source: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-C1y68LXKNk>

1.  Go to a channel page (<https://www.youtube.com/@CaptainSparklez2>)
2.  Right click and \"View Page Source\"
3.  Search for \"RSS\"
4.  RSS link will look like
    <https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UCAJ41d46i6mcNAsvsWRc9hw>
