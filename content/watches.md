# GShock #
- 2100 line is colloquially known as the Casioak
    - similarity to Audemars Piguet - Royal Oak, octagonal bezel
        - though GShock has always had an octagonal bezel, albeit non-regular
    - GA- is basic line, resin
    - B suffix adds bluetooth time-sync and solar recharge
- It seems that positive displays are more legible than negative
    - positive: dark text on light background (e.g. DW5600BCE-1)
    - negative: light text on dark background (e.g. DW5600BB-1)
    - positive is the standard type
